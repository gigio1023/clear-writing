# Readability Gates

Run this before delivery.

## Gate 1: Main task

- Is the main user problem solved first?
- Would a reader know why this document exists within the first few lines?

## Gate 2: Concrete language

- Did label-heavy sentences get rewritten into mechanism or observable detail?
- Are placeholder actors reduced where the source supports it?

## Gate 3: Standalone context

- Can a new reader follow the document without the original conversation?
- Are assumptions, unknowns, and open questions visible where needed?

## Gate 4: Rhythm and trust

- Does the prose vary in length and cadence naturally?
- Does it avoid consultant tone, coaching tone, and slogan-like phrasing?
- Does it respect the reader instead of overexplaining obvious points?

## Gate 5: Density

- Did you cut filler without cutting necessary reasoning?
- Is every section earning its space?

## Gate 6: Safety

- Were facts preserved?
- Were citations, dates, and uncertainty markers kept intact?
- Were domain terms preserved when they were actually correct?
